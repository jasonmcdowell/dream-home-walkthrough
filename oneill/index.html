The compass uses 0° for the +Z endcap and increases toward spinward; −Z is the opposite endcap. At the axis tram, press E or Y / Triangle to ride to an end-cap airlock. Leave the open doors clear and follow the tunnel outward into zero-G space, up to 1,000 m from the hull. Outside, Space or A / Cross rises and Ctrl or B / Circle lowers. While flying inside, pass through the axis to the opposite side; your view stays upside down until you leave flight and land, when gravity turns the ground down. Press H, R1, or Options again to close.
